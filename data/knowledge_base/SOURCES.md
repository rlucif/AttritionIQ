# Sources for the HR policy handbook

Every value in `hr_policy_handbook.md` is either taken from one of these sources or marked "company choice" in the text. Checked 2026-10-08 against the primary texts.

## Law

| Handbook rule | Source |
|---|---|
| Max 48 hours a week on average, including overtime | Directive 2003/88/EC (Working Time Directive), Art. 6(b). https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32003L0088 |
| Averaged over up to four months | Same, Art. 16(b) |
| 11 hours daily rest; 24 + 11 hours weekly rest | Same, Art. 3 and Art. 5 |
| 5% gender pay gap, six months to fix, then joint pay assessment | Directive (EU) 2023/970 (Pay Transparency Directive), Art. 10(1). https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2023/970/oj |
| Right not to be subject to solely automated decisions | Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR), Art. 22(1). https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj |
| Promotion, termination, performance-monitoring AI is high-risk | Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (AI Act), Annex III point 4(b). https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj |
| Inform worker representatives and workers before use | Same, Art. 26(7) |
| Two flexible-working requests per 12 months, decision within two months, consult before refusing | Employment Relations (Flexible Working) Act 2023 (UK), explanatory notes. https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2023/33/notes/division/2/index.htm |

## Company practice (GitLab public handbook)

GitLab publishes its internal handbook openly; it is used here as a documented example of real HR practice, not as law.

| Handbook rule | GitLab page |
|---|---|
| Semi-annual calibrated promotion process; quarterly calibration | Promotions and Transfers. https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/people-group/promotions-transfers/ |
| Six months in current role before applying internally | Same page |
| Weekly 1-1s; engagement check-in at least every six months | 1-1. https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/leadership/1-1/ |
| Range midpoint at 50th percentile (75th for critical technical roles); minimum = 80% of midpoint | Compensation. https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/total-rewards/compensation/ |
| RSUs vest over two to four years | Stock Options / Equity. https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/total-rewards/stock-options/ |
| Up to US$10,000 a year learning fund after three months; not while on a PIP; travel excluded | Growth and Development Fund. https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/total-rewards/benefits/general-and-entity-benefits/growth-and-development |

## Company choices (no external source; the team owns these)

Four consecutive weeks of overtime triggers a review; up to two days time off in lieu per quarter; promotion discussion after three years without one; off-cycle promotion needs department head + HR; off-cycle pay rise up to 10% by department head; cash retention bonus option; equity nomination for stock level 0; priority for people with no training last year; mentoring in first two years; up to three remote days a week; travel reduction on request; internal advertising first; rotation after three years in role, for six to twelve months; quarterly exit-theme reporting; no protected characteristics in risk scores.
